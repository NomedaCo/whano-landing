# Legal Page Notes

The site includes Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Data Processing Agreement
pages in English and formal Arabic. They describe the current product behavior:
Shopify order data, WhatsApp messaging through a shared WhatsApp Business Account,
message processing, points-based billing, third-party infrastructure, deletion and
retention, and merchant responsibility for customer contact consent.

2026-10-24 updates:

- Public contact is `contact@nomeda.tech`; privacy requests use
  `privacy@nomeda.tech`.
- The DPA lists Bosta and ShipBlu (conditional), Meta/WhatsApp, Shopify, the AI
  model provider, Resend, Railway, and Cloudflare.
- Privacy now names marketing consent/opt-out state and the campaign/cart-recovery
  use; retention distinguishes the 48-hour post-uninstall redaction path from a
  separate deletion request (up to 30 days).
- The legal entity in these documents is NomedaCo; the public brand is Nomeda.

These pages are product-facing documents, not a substitute for legal advice.
Before public launch or a material product change, a qualified lawyer should
review the final text against the operating company, data-processing locations,
WhatsApp/Meta terms, Shopify protected-customer-data rules, and applicable
Egyptian and international privacy requirements.
