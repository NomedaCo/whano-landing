# Legal Page Notes

The site includes Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages in English and formal Arabic. They describe the current product behavior: Shopify order data, WhatsApp messaging infrastructure, message processing, points-based pricing, third-party infrastructure, deletion requests, and merchant responsibility for customer contact consent.

The pages are product-facing documents, not a substitute for legal advice. Before public launch, a qualified lawyer should review the final text against the operating company, data-processing locations, WhatsApp integration terms, and applicable Egyptian and international privacy requirements.
