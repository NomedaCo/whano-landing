# Product Website Strategy

## Positioning

Whano automates the WhatsApp conversation around a Shopify order — confirmation,
edits, cancellation, shipping, delivery, and reviews — without a number to connect.

## Audience

Egyptian Shopify merchants selling physical products, especially fashion and
accessories, cash-on-delivery sellers, and owner-run stores where the same person
handles orders, WhatsApp, and support. The site is Arabic-first; English is
available for the Shopify listing audience and international reviewers.

## Five-second message

Whano connects Shopify to WhatsApp and handles the order after checkout:
confirmation, changes, delivery updates, and reviews — with no number setup and
one-time points instead of a monthly subscription.

## Primary conversion

Install on Shopify (`https://apps.shopify.com/whano`). Secondary: log in to the
console. Centralized in `src/data/content.ts`.

## Verified differentiators

- No number to link: a store number is provisioned inside Whano's WhatsApp
  Business Account; no Meta API account or device to maintain.
- Arabic-first product and customer messages; the console is Arabic for merchants.
- One-time points packs from 499 EGP (450 points) to 2,999 EGP (3,500
  points), paid through Shopify or customer support with InstaPay or Vodafone
  Cash; no monthly subscription, no automatic renewal, points never expire.
- Confirmation and cancellation keep working at zero balance.
- Bosta and ShipBlu shipping, tracking, exceptions, pickup where supported, and
  the customer order page.
- Abandoned-cart recovery and a consent-aware campaign audience (order count,
  spend, bulk add/remove, block list, optional auto opt-in).
- Product messages, size verification, message templates, reminder/auto-cancel,
  quiet mode, and push/reports/exports.
- Human support handoff through support tickets / the Whano team.
- A live order page that answers "where is my order?" without an app or account.

## Objections addressed

- Do I need a number or Meta account? No on the normal path; explained in the FAQ.
- Will the Arabic sound robotic? The customer messages are Egyptian colloquial and
  the merchant controls templates/settings.
- Can customers change orders? Yes, before fulfillment, through buttons or
  a secure one-time Edit order link; Shopify stays current.
- What happens at zero points? Confirmation and cancellation continue; optional
  automations pause.
- Does it work with my carrier? Bosta and ShipBlu create shipments and tracking;
  other carriers can still receive a tracking update through Shopify.
- What if a person is needed? A support ticket brings a person in; the merchant
  sees the full message log (merchant chats are read-only).
- Can I run several stores in one console? Not for merchants; the multi-store
  view is a Whano team tool. Do not claim it.
- How is usage priced? Transparent points packs; a full journey is about 4 points.

## Space we deliberately leave

The site does not invent customer counts, review scores beyond the live listing,
customer logos, performance guarantees, discounts, or carrier support. It does
not claim merchant chat takeover, merchant multi-store, or teammate invites.
