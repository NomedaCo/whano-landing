# Anti-Slop Review

## Prohibited patterns

- Generic purple/blue AI gradients.
- Decorative blobs, floating shapes, glassmorphism, or glowing borders.
- A grid of interchangeable feature cards with decorative icons.
- Fake testimonials, customer logos, usage numbers, or urgency.
- Buzzwords such as `revolutionize`, `supercharge`, and `seamlessly`.
- Emoji-led marketing copy.
- A generic dashboard screenshot that could belong to any SaaS product.
- A dead CTA URL or a claim that the App Store listing is live when it is not.

## Review questions

1. Does the section explain a real part of the Shopify order lifecycle?
2. Could the copy belong to any other automation product?
3. Is the claim supported by the actual product or clearly labeled as an example?
4. Does Arabic sound like Egyptian product language rather than literal translation?
5. Does the layout still work without decorative effects?
6. Is there one obvious next action?
