# Anti-AI-Slop Checklist

## What IS AI Slop

Generic, soulless, immediately recognizable as "generated." The kind of landing page where you can smell the prompt.

## Patterns to Avoid

### Visual
- [ ] Rainbow/iridescent gradients (the "AI gradient" of 2024-2026)
- [ ] Floating 3D blobs or glassmorphism overuse
- [ ] Generic "hero screenshot" with fake data
- [ ] Purple-to-blue gradient backgrounds (overused by every AI tool)
- [ ] Glowing borders on every card
- [ ] Stock photos of people smiling at laptops
- [ ] Emoji in headlines, CTAs, or feature descriptions
- [ ] Heavy drop shadows on every card
- [ ] Animated particles or "digital rain" backgrounds

### Copy
- [ ] "Revolutionize", "supercharge", "empower", "transform"
- [ ] "AI-powered" as a standalone feature (say what it DOES)
- [ ] "Join 10,000+ happy customers" (fake numbers)
- [ ] "Unlimited free trial!" (misleading)
- [ ] "The future of [thing]" (vague)
- [ ] "Seamlessly integrate" (means nothing)
- [ ] "Leverage cutting-edge technology" (buzzword soup)
- [ ] Exclamation marks on every sentence!!!
- [ ] "World-class" anything

### Structure
- [ ] 6 identical feature cards in a grid with icons
- [ ] Generic testimonial carousel with stock avatars
- [ ] "As seen on" logo bar with irrelevant logos
- [ ] Countdown timer creating fake urgency
- [ ] "Only X spots left!" (fabricated scarcity)

## What We Do Instead

| AI Slop | Whano Approach |
|---|---|
| "Revolutionize your customer communication" | "Your customers text WhatsApp. Whano replies." |
| Purple gradient hero with fake screenshot | Dark background with animated WhatsApp conversation |
| 6 feature cards with icons | Full-width alternating sections with live mockups |
| "Join thousands of happy merchants" | "Built for Egyptian Shopify merchants" |
| "AI-powered magic" | "Confirms orders in 3 seconds. In Arabic." |
| Stock photo of team | Product mockup showing real conversations |
| Emoji in CTAs | Plain text: "Install on Shopify" |
| "Start your free trial today" | "50 free messages. No credit card." |
| Countdown timer | Honest pricing with EGP amounts |

## Quality Gates

Before shipping any section, check:
1. Does this look like it was made by a human who cares?
2. Would I be embarrassed if a designer saw this?
3. Is every word earning its place?
4. Does this show the product, not just talk about it?
5. Is this specific to Whano, or could it be any SaaS?
