## Features

### 1. Order Confirmation
Every order gets a WhatsApp confirmation in seconds. Customer texts "بتأكيد الطلب" (or just replies with a checkmark), and the order is confirmed. No app to install, no link to click.

### 2. Smart Recommendations
After confirmation, Whano suggests related products based on what other customers actually bought together. Customer says "add the blue one" — Whano updates the real Shopify order.

### 3. Shipping Updates
When you fulfill an order, Whano sends the tracking info automatically. Customer gets a message with delivery window and tracking link. No "where is my order?" messages.

### 4. Review Collection
24-48 hours after delivery, Whano asks for a review. One message, one reply. Reviews stored in your dashboard. No third-party review app needed.

### 5. Bilingual Support
Speaks Egyptian Arabic and English. Detects customer language from their Shopify profile. Responds naturally in the right dialect — not generic MSA, not robotic English.
